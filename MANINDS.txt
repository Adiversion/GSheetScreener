Your FTF logic says:

```text

        ↓
```

But history says:

```text
₹944
 ↓
BROKEN
 ↓
₹1,005
 ↓

```

Therefore the state machine needs **pivot lifecycle management**.

A resistance pivot should not remain a resistance pivot forever after it has been decisively cleared.

I'd implement:

```text
PIVOT_944
   │
   ├── rejected → RESISTANCE
   │
   ├── cleared + accepted → BROKEN_RESISTANCE
   │
   └── later retest → SUPPORT_RETEST
```

So MANINDS should have become:

### `SUPPORT_RETEST`

rather than:

### `RESISTANCE_PROBE`

after the 23-Sep breakout.


I think MANINDS reveals that you need one additional state:

# `POST_BREAKOUT_RETEST`

The state transition becomes:

```text
RESISTANCE
    │
    │ breakout + acceptance
    ▼
CONFIRMED_BREAKOUT
    │
    │ pullback
    ▼
POST_BREAKOUT_RETEST
    │
    ├── support holds
    │      ↓
    │  CONTINUATION_READY
    │
    └── support fails
           ↓
       FAILED_BREAKOUT
```

This is much more powerful than continually treating the original resistance as an entry trigger.
