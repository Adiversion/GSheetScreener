An independent technical and quantitative critique of this engine reveals a sound foundational framework borrowed from classical trend-following, alongside several structural flaws, statistical oversights, and implementation risks specific to the Indian equity markets.

---

### Strongest Counterargument (The Core Flaw)

The primary vulnerability of this system lies in **Milestone 3: Full Take Profit at +50%**.

Trend-following and Stage-2 momentum strategies (Minervini, O'Neil, Donchian) derive their entire mathematical edge from **positive skewness** (a fat right tail where a tiny fraction of trades yield 200% to 500%+ gains to offset numerous small losses and breakevens). Capping upside at +50% truncates the distribution's right tail, transforming an asymmetric trend-following model into a high-churn swing-trading model with an inferior payoff ratio. In a choppy or rotational market, repeated -7% cuts will bleed the portfolio, while in a structural bull run, the engine cuts winners prematurely just as institutional accumulation enters its parabolic phase.

### Strategy-by-Strategy Technical Audit

| # | Strategy Component | Mechanical Assessment & Limitations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Stage-2 Trend Template** | **Sound, but late.** Requiring $Price > SMA_{50} > SMA_{150} > SMA_{200}$ alongside a rising $SMA_{200}$ is robust for trend confirmation, but it is inherently lagging. By the time this alignment solidifies, 30%–60% of the initial Stage 2 move off a base has already occurred. |
| **2** | **Compound Momentum Score (CMS)** | **Dimensional mismatch & redundant math.** <br>

<br>1. *Formula redundancy:* $(1 - \frac{52W_{High} - CMP}{52W_{High}}) \times 100 = \frac{CMP}{52W_{High}} \times 100$. Wrapping it in a two-step subtraction adds no informational value.<br>

<br>2. *Unit mismatch:* $ROC_{3M}$ is an unscaled percentage (often ranging from -20% to +100%+), while Proximity Score is bounded strictly between 0 and 100. Adding these directly without cross-sectional percentile normalization means an extreme 3-month runner will distort the composite weight far beyond the nominal 60/40 ratio. |
| **3** | **Base Extension & ATR Cap** | **Contradictory filters.** A maximum $Dist_{50} \le 25\%$ protects against buying overbought peaks. However, coupling this with a requirement that the stock be within 25% of its 52-week high while $SMA_{50} > SMA_{150} > SMA_{200}$ creates a narrow band. Furthermore, an ATR ceiling of $\le 5.0\%$ will systematically eliminate the highest-beta mid- and small-cap leaders on the NSE, which frequently trade at higher daily ATRs during breakout regimes. |
| **4** | **RSI (14) Sweet Zone** | **Capping RSI at 75 penalizes true power trends.** Classical Minervini/CANSLIM leaders frequently sustain daily RSI readings between 75 and 85 during their most aggressive markup phases. An upper limit of 75 triggers false negatives on the strongest institutional momentum names. |
| **5** | **Liquidity Threshold** | **Turnover $\ge$ ₹2 Crore is too low for zero slippage.** An entity trading ₹2 Crore ($20\text{ million INR}$) daily can only support position sizes of ₹2–5 Lakh without moving the bid-ask spread. For institutional scaling or accounts compounding past ₹50 Lakh, this threshold creates severe market-impact slippage, especially on lower-circuit days. |
| **6** | **Macro Regime Guard** | **Nifty 50 is the wrong benchmark.** Small- and mid-caps regularly diverge from the large-cap Nifty 50 index for quarters at a time. The **Nifty Midcap 150** or **Nifty 500** provides a significantly more accurate regime signal for an all-NSE stock pool. |
| **7** | **Exit System (GTT)** | **Premature profit lock and execution risk:**<br>

<br>• Moving stops to +2.5% at +15% gain ignores the normal volatility of Indian mid-caps. A standard 10–12% base-retest will routinely stop the position out at +2.5% before the real trend develops.<br>

<br>• GTT orders on NSE execute as market orders once triggered; during gap-downs or lower circuits (common in high-momentum Indian equities), a -7% GTT stop offers no guarantee of being filled at ₹930. |

### Critical NSE-Specific Realities Overlooked

1. **Circuit Limits & Liquidity Traps:**
Many stocks passing the ₹2 Crore / 100k volume filter sit in 5% or 10% daily price bands. If momentum reverses, stocks frequently hit lower circuits back-to-back, rendering GTT and stop-loss rules physically non-executable until losses far exceed -7%.
2. **Survivorship & Corporate Action Biases:**
Scanning 2,600+ NSE stocks requires point-in-time historical data adjusted for splits, bonuses, and rights issues. Evaluating unadjusted 52-week highs and moving averages across non-survivorship-free databases generates phantom Stage 2 breakouts.
3. **Tax & Churn Drag:**
Targeting +15% to +50% swings with strict ratchets creates high portfolio turnover. Under the Indian tax framework (STCG at 20% post-July 2024 budget, plus STT, stamp duty, exchange turnover charges, and GST), frequent rebalancing places a heavy hurdle on net portfolio CAGR.

---

### Quantitative Recommendations

* **Normalize Momentum Scores:** Standardize $ROC_{3M}$ and Proximity via cross-sectional percentile ranks ($Percentile(ROC_{3M}) \times 0.60 + Percentile(Proximity) \times 0.40$) to enforce consistent factor exposures.
* **Remove the Hard +50% Ceiling:** Replace Milestone 3 with a dynamic trend-following exit, such as an ATR trailing stop or a confirmed close below the 50-day SMA, allowing outsized outliers to compound uninterrupted.
* **Align Macro Benchmark:** Replace Nifty 50 with the Nifty 500 or Nifty MidSmallcap 400 to reflect the broader breadth of the eligible 2,600-stock universe.
* **Raise Liquidity Floor:** Increase the minimum average daily turnover to at least ₹5–10 Crore to absorb trade execution and limit circuit-lock exposure.
