## The deeper conclusion

What you've discovered across these four stocks is **not simply that the new trap detector works**.

You've discovered that the engine needs to maintain **three separate histories**:

```text
                    ┌──────────────┐
                    │     SQS      │
                    │ What is this │
                    │ stock?       │
                    └──────┬───────┘
                           │

                    │ event doing? │
                    └──────┬───────┘
                           │
                ┌──────────┴──────────┐
                │                     │
           ENTRY ACTION          NO ENTRY
```

And within ECS, **pivots and floors themselves need lifecycle states**.

That is the architectural direction I'd take now.

**I would not add another indicator yet.** The four case studies are exposing state-transition and temporal-semantics issues that are much more important than adding another AQS component.
