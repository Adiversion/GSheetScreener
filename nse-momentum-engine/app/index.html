Strategy & glossary

Rotation plan — +15% cycles, one capital base

  • Initial stop (−5% to −7% ATR): 2×ATR₁₄ bounded between −5% and −7%, tightened to −4% in defensive regimes. Hard tail-risk protection.
  • Target (+15%): the entire position is sold at +15%. In rotation mode there is no partial runner and no trailing — the target is the exit.
  • Redeploy: sell proceeds (principal + profit) buy the next ranked leader. No new capital is ever added; only the first ₹1,000 is funded.
  • Optional trailing (advanced): if you choose not to rotate, you can instead bank 40% at +22% and trail the remaining 60% below the 50-SMA / 20-EMA. The terminal shows both, but the rotation plan is the default.

Gap-ups and the 3% max-chase rule

  • 0–3% gap (safe): normal open. Enter your real fill price — the ticket recalculates every level from it.
  • +3% to +5% (extended): chasing raises pullback risk. Wait for a dip or take a smaller quantity.
  • > +5% / circuit (do not chase): never chase an over-extended gap or locked circuit. Switch to the next alternate.
