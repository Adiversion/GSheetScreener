🎯 Two-Tier Profit Booking & Multibagger Ride System
  • Initial Stop Loss (-5% to -7% ATR): Emergency downside boundary ($2 \times \text{ATR}_{14}$ bounded between -5% and -7%, tightened to -4% in defensive regimes). Hard protection against tail risk.
  • M1 Target (+15% Risk-Free): First milestone. When price reaches +15%, your GTT stop loss is automatically ratcheted up to +1.5% Breakeven Buffer (`M1_STOP`) to cover all exchange charges, STT, and stamp duty. 100% position remains active and completely risk-free!
  • M2 Target (+22% Bank & Trail): Second milestone. Bank 40% partial profit to lock in a $\ge 3:1$ ($3R$) payoff. Simultaneously ratchet stop loss on the remaining 60% runner to +10% locked profit floor.
  • M3 Multibagger Runner (> +25% Uncapped): No rigid profit cap! Let your 60% runner ride into 100% to 500%+ institutional trends. Trailed dynamically via: (1) 50-day SMA on volume (1-day grace on low vol), (2) 20-day EMA climax exit when extended > 35% above 50 SMA, and (3) Blow-off exhaustion exit into peak volume.
⚡ What if the Stock Gaps Up at 9:15 AM?
When market opens, a high-momentum stock may gap up +2% to +5% above yesterday's close:
  • 0% to +3% Gap (🟢 Safe Zone): Normal opening. Enter your actual fill price in the app — it recalculates your exact stops and targets from your fill price!
  • +3% to +5% Gap (🟡 Extended): Chasing increases pullback risk. Wait 15 minutes for a dip or take smaller quantity.
  • > +5% / Upper Circuit (🔴 Do NOT Chase): Never chase an over-extended gap or locked circuit! Immediately switch to Alternate #1 (Rank #2).
📚 Jargon Dictionary
CMP: Current Market Price (latest closing price on NSE).
CMS Score: Cross-Sectional Percentile Rank blending 3M momentum speed and 52W high breakout proximity.
RSI (14): Relative Strength Index. 45–82 is the institutional momentum sweet spot.
ATR (14): Average True Range. Average daily price movement in rupees.
SMA 50 / 200: 50-day and 200-day Simple Moving Average. Price must stay above both for Stage-2 markup.
GTT: Good Till Triggered. A 1-year standing order on Zerodha/Groww that automatically sells when your stop or target is hit.
