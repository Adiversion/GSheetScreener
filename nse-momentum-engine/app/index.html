📖 Strategy & Jargon Guide
🎯 The 3-Milestone Exit System
  • Initial Stop Loss (-7%): Automatic emergency exit. If price drops 7% below entry, GTT sells instantly. Limits maximum loss to ₹70 on ₹1,000.
  • M1 Target (+15%): First milestone! When price reaches +15%, your GTT stop loss is automatically ratcheted up to +2.5% Breakeven (`M1_STOP`). You are now in a risk-free trade!
  • M2 Target (+30%): Second milestone. Your trailing stop loss moves up to +15% (`M2_STOP`), locking in at least +15% profit in your bank even if the market reverses.
  • M3 Target (+50%): Final multibagger take-profit level! Full exit. Your entire capital (principal + 50% profit) is rolled into the next #1 momentum winner to compound.
⚡ What if the Stock Gaps Up at 9:15 AM?
When market opens, a high-momentum stock may gap up +2% to +5% above yesterday's close:
  • 0% to +3% Gap (🟢 Safe Zone): Normal opening. Enter your actual fill price in the app — it recalculates your exact -7% stop and +15% targets from your fill price!
  • +3% to +5% Gap (🟡 Extended): Chasing increases pullback risk. Wait 15 minutes for a dip or take smaller quantity.
  • > +5% / Upper Circuit (🔴 Do NOT Chase): Never chase an over-extended gap or locked circuit! Immediately switch to Alternate #1 (Rank #2).
🏆 How to Pick When Multiple Stocks Qualify
Today the screener found Stage-2 leaders. Here is how to handle them:
  • ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 Capital (Single-Bullet Compounding): Put 100% into the #1 Ranked Winner that fits your budget. Spreading small capital causes excess DP charges (₹15.93 per exit) that eat profits.
  • ₹10,000+ Capital (Basket Diversification): Split capital equally across Top 3 (33% each) or Top 5 (20% each).
  • Already own #1 or #1 is at Upper Circuit? Take Alternate #1 (Rank #2).
📚 Jargon Dictionary
CMP: Current Market Price (latest closing price on NSE).
CMS: Compound Momentum Score (0-100+ composite score ranking 1M, 2M, 3M speed and 52W high breakout proximity).
RSI (14): Relative Strength Index. 50–70 is the institutional momentum sweet spot.
ATR (14): Average True Range. The average daily price movement in rupees.
SMA 50 / 200: 50-day and 200-day Simple Moving Average. Price must stay above both for Stage-2 markup.
GTT: Good Till Triggered. A 1-year standing order on Zerodha/Groww that automatically sells when your stop or target is hit.
CNC: Cash and Carry. Equity delivery order where you own 100% of shares with zero leverage or liquidation risk.
