
```text

    ↓

    ↓
BREAKOUT
```

WELSPUNLIV looks much closer to **Scenario B** in hindsight.

Therefore the correct action isn't:

# 2. Measure "rejection efficiency"

This is something I would add to USER_V25.

Suppose a stock has a large rejection.

Calculate:

```text
RejectionMagnitude =
    High - Close
```

Then ask:

```text
ForwardDownside =
    Close[t] - minimum(Close[t+1:t+3])
```

If:

```text
BIG rejection
+
BIG volume
+
very little subsequent downside
```

then the original rejection did **not produce much downside price displacement**.

That's more interesting than simply saying:

```text
volume = 4x
```

# 5. The most important new feature: "Failure to Fail"

I would actually create this as a formal USER_V25 feature.

Call it:

### FTF — Failure-to-Fail Score

The idea:

> A bearish-looking event becomes less bearish when price repeatedly refuses to follow through downward.

For example:

```text
Large rejection
      ↓
Expected downside
      ↓
Did downside happen?
      ↓
       NO
      ↓
Price recovers
      ↓
Retests resistance
```

FTF becomes high.

Mathematically:

```text
FTF =
    f(
      rejection magnitude,
      rejection volume,
      downside follow-through,
      support retention,
      recovery speed,
      resistance retest
    )
```

**Do not try to predict whether the 30-Sep candle is a trap.**
